# switchyard

### screenshots!



what is this game?

- you're controlling railway switches.
- trains constantly enter the map, and you have to route them to the correct destinations.
- your job is to switch the tracks where the intersection is at the right time

### how can i play this?

- you can find the demo on my itch.io!
